# DayScore Vision: Goal & Success Criteria

## Overall Goal
Transform DayScore from a data collection tool into a life intelligence system that tells you what your own data means — surfacing patterns, contradictions, and cause-and-effect chains you can't see from the inside.

**Success metric**: You make a different decision at least once per week because of something DayScore surfaced that you wouldn't have noticed on your own.

---

## Phase 1: Life Score 2.0
Replace the 0-6 checkbox counter with four dimension scores (Body, Mind, Spirit, Heart) that show where you're thriving and where you're declining.

**Success**: You look at the dashboard and immediately know which dimension needs attention this week — without reading any entries.

## Phase 2: Intention-Outcome Engine
Close the loop between morning priorities and evening outcomes. Track follow-through rate by category.

**Success**: Your follow-through rate is visible and trending upward. Stuck items get unstuck because the system flags them before you'd notice.

## Phase 3: Relationship Intelligence
Turn nightly nightcap data into a multi-pillar relationship health dashboard.

**Success**: You and Catherine can see which relationship dimensions are strong and which are drifting — and act before it becomes a conversation problem.

## Phase 4: Temporal Pattern Engine
Discover your personal cause-and-effect chains from historical data.

**Success**: The system surfaces a non-obvious pattern (something you didn't already know) that holds up when you test it.

## Phase 5: Life Intelligence Engine
AI synthesis layer that reads everything and produces weekly intelligence briefs, morning context, and blind spot reports.

**Success**: The weekly brief replaces the current digest and you actually look forward to reading it because it tells you something new every time.
